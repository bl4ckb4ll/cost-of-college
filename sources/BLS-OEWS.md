# BLS Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics

Primary source: https://www.bls.gov/oes/

The Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics program publishes annual employment and wage estimates for roughly 830 occupations. Estimates are available nationally, by state, by metropolitan/nonmetropolitan area, and nationally by industry.

SOC system: https://www.bls.gov/soc/

The current OEWS occupation structure uses the 2018 Standard Occupational Classification system. A hypothetical family's occupation record should retain the detailed SOC code rather than only a free-text job title.

## Wage fields to retain

Where available:

- employment
- mean annual wage
- mean hourly wage
- 10th percentile
- 25th percentile
- median
- 75th percentile
- 90th percentile
- geography
- source year
- SOC code

## Important limitation

OEWS wages are straight-time gross pay. The survey excludes overtime premium pay, shift differentials, severance pay, nonproduction bonuses, employer supplementary benefits, and tuition reimbursement. Therefore overtime must be represented separately as an explicit family assumption or another sourced input.
