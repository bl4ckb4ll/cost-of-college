# Single-parent flight-attendant family

A 17-year-old student in Michigan lives with their mother. The parents are separated. The student's father contributes no money toward college.

The mother works as a flight attendant. For this prototype, her occupational earnings are set to the May 2025 national BLS median annual wage for flight attendants, SOC 53-2031: $63,580.

The story assumes no taxable income besides wages and no adjustments to income. Under those explicit assumptions, calculated AGI is also $63,580.

The mother says that, regardless of what an aid formula concludes she can afford, she can actually provide $2,000 per year toward college. The other parent provides $0.

The question for each college is therefore not simply "what is the published net price?" It is:

> After grants and scholarships, how much money must this student actually produce, where could that money come from, and does attending require debt or resources that this family does not have?

## Important time alignment

This is currently a family-generator prototype, not a 2026-27 FAFSA result. The measured wage input is from 2025. The 2026-27 FAFSA uses 2024 federal tax information, so an aid calculation must wait until the family's tax inputs are aligned to the relevant tax year.
